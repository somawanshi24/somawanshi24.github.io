SQL fraud risk scoring system

Problem
A naive rule-based flag caught only 16 of 8,213 frauds in 6.3M synthetic transactions (0.19% recall).
Approach
Built a four-rule weighted risk scoring UDF, views and a stored procedure with balance validation and rollback in MySQL. Designed without the fraud label to avoid data leakage.
Result
Fraud recall rose from 0.19% to 94.36% against the baseline. A stricter 5-rule threshold was tested, and the high-recall version recommended. Visualised in Tableau.
